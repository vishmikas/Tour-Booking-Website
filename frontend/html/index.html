Welcome to Misty Mount Travels, your trusted travel partner in the heart of Sri Lanka's hill country. We are a locally owned, government-licensed tour operator dedicated to showcasing the beauty of Nuwara Eliya.

Unlike app-based services, we own our fleet of vehicles and employ local drivers who know every curve of these mountain roads. Whether you are a solo backpacker or a family on vacation, we prioritize your safety and comfort above all else.
